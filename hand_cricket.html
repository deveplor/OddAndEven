📖 Hand Cricket Official Rules

1. The Toss Phase

Choose Odd or Even and pick a number from 1 to 10. The AI simultaneously picks a random number from 1 to 10. If the sum of both numbers matches your Odd/Even call, you win the toss and choose whether to Bat or Bowl first. Otherwise, the AI wins the toss and randomly decides.

2. 1st Innings (Setting the Target)

Each ball, both you and the AI choose an integer from 1 to 10. If the numbers are different, the batter's number is added to their total score. If both numbers match exactly, the batter is OUT! The target for the 2nd innings becomes 1st Innings Score + 1.

3. 2nd Innings (The Chase)

Roles swap! The chasing side bats to reach the Target. If their accumulated score meets or surpasses the Target, they instantly win. If the bowler matches the batter's number before the Target is reached, the chasing side is OUT and the defending side wins!

4. Match Drawn (Tie) Rules

If the chasing side in the 2nd Innings is bowled OUT when their score is exactly equal to the 1st Innings score (e.g. 42 vs 42, just 1 run short of the target 43), the match ends as an official MATCH DRAWN (TIE)! In Multiplayer, both players' Elo ratings remain unchanged (0 Elo delta). In Bot matches, the match is recorded as a Draw with ratings frozen.
