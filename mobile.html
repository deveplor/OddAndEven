📖 Official Hand Cricket Rules

1. Toss: Call Odd or Even and pick a number (1–10). If the sum matches your call, you choose to Bat or Bowl first.

2. Scoring: Both players pick numbers (1–10). If numbers are different, the batter scores their runs. If numbers match exactly, the batter is OUT!

3. The Chase: In Innings 2, roles swap. If the chasing team reaches the target, they win!

4. Match Drawn (Tie): If the chasing team is bowled OUT when their score is exactly equal to the 1st Innings score (e.g. 5 vs 5, 1 run away from target 6), the match is an official MATCH DRAWN! In Multiplayer, both players' ratings remain unchanged (0 Elo change). In Bot matches, the match is drawn with ratings frozen.

5. 1v1 & 2v2 Squad Multiplayer Rules: Choose 1v1 Duel (2 players, 1 wicket) or 2v2 Squad Match (4 players: invite 3 friends, pick 1 teammate for Your Team and remaining 2 join Opponent Team). You are Your Team's Captain and can assign the Opponent Team Captain. Captains perform the Toss and choose Bat or Bowl first. In 2v2, the bowler switches every 6 balls (1 Over), and in batting when 1 player gets OUT the 2nd comes in — when both get OUT (2/2), the Opponent Team gets the Target!

6. Leaderboard & Stats Eligibility: Matches against bots are Casual Practice: runs, wickets, match wins, and losses are not counted against bots and are never displayed in the Leaderboard. Only official Multiplayer matches (1v1 & 2v2) count toward career runs, wickets, wins, losses, and Leaderboard standings!
