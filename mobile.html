📖 Official Hand Cricket Rules

1. Toss: Call Odd or Even and pick a number (1–10). If the sum matches your call, you choose to Bat or Bowl first.

2. Scoring: Both players pick numbers (1–10). If numbers are different, the batter scores their runs. If numbers match exactly, the batter is OUT!

3. The Chase: In Innings 2, roles swap. If the chasing team reaches the target, they win!

4. Match Drawn (Tie): If the chasing team is bowled OUT when their score is exactly equal to the 1st Innings score (e.g. 5 vs 5, 1 run away from target 6), the match is an official MATCH DRAWN! In Multiplayer, both players' ratings remain unchanged (0 Elo change). In Bot matches, the match is drawn with ratings frozen.

5. Ratings: Multiplayer wins/losses earn or lose ±8 Elo. Matches against bots are Casual Practice (Ratings Frozen)!
