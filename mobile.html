📖 Official Hand Cricket Rules

1. Toss: Call Odd or Even and pick a number (1–10). If the sum matches your call, you choose to Bat or Bowl first.

2. Scoring: Both players pick numbers (1–10). If numbers are different, the batter scores their runs. If numbers match exactly, the batter is OUT!

3. The Chase: In Innings 2, roles swap. If the chasing team reaches the target, they win!
