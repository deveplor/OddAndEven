      How the match works

      Choose 1–6 when it is your turn. Matching numbers mean a wicket; otherwise the batter scores their number. Both choices lock before the ball is revealed. In 2v2, the second batter enters after a wicket and bowlers alternate every six balls. The toss-winning captain chooses bat or bowl. Chase the target before losing all wickets or using all overs.

      These are friendly peer-to-peer matches, saved to your local room history. They do not award ranked XP. The host must keep the page open. Some restricted networks may block peer connections; a failed connection will never count as a joined player.
