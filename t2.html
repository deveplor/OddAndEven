⚡ How the 16-Player Double Elimination (32 Matches) Works

Online Fixtures Only · No Fixed Players
1. WINNERS BRACKET (15 MATCHES)

All 16 online slots start in Winners Round 1 (M1–M8). Winners stay in the Winners Bracket through Quarterfinals (M13–M16), Semifinals (M21–M22), and the Winners Final (M27).

2. FIRST LOSS → LOSERS BRACKET (14 MATCHES)

The first time an online contender loses in the Winners Bracket, they are not eliminated—they drop into the Losers Bracket (M9–M12, M17–M20, M23–M26, M28–M29).

3. SECOND LOSS ELIMINATION & PODIUM (M30)

Any contender suffering a loss inside the Losers Bracket is eliminated upon their 2nd tournament defeat. The final two Losers Bracket contenders contest Match #30 (3rd Place Podium Decider).

4. GRAND FINAL & BRACKET RESET (M31 & M32)

The undefeated Winners Champion faces the Losers Champion in M31 (Grand Final). If the Losers Champion wins M31 (giving the WB Champ their 1st loss), M32 (Bracket Reset) decides the title!
